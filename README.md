# FIRSTPOD

A curated collection of insights on software, AI, systems, and the ideas shaping the world.

![FIRSTPOD Homepage](assets/firstpod-home.png)

## About

FIRSTPOD is a digital publication exploring the intersection of technology, security, and engineering. Each post goes beyond surface-level coverage to examine the underlying principles, trade-offs, and practical lessons that matter to practitioners.

Topics include:

- **Cybersecurity** — Threat landscapes, incident analysis, and defensive strategies
- **Software Engineering** — Architecture, delivery practices, and technical decision-making
- **AI Systems** — Practical applications, infrastructure, and operational considerations
- **Distributed Systems** — Resilience, failure modes, and design patterns

## Philosophy

Good technical writing should respect the reader's time while challenging their assumptions. FIRSTPOD aims to be concise without being superficial, opinionated without being dismissive, and practical without being reductive.
